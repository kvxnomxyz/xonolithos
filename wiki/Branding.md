# Branding

The xOS mark is a faceted X in ice blue and violet. The [vector logo](https://github.com/kvxnomxyz/xonolithos/blob/main/branding/xonolithos-logo.svg) is the canonical source; the PNG is an export.

The [boot animation source](https://github.com/kvxnomxyz/xonolithos/blob/main/scripts/make-bootanimation.py) draws a small phone, breaks it into shards, reveals **XonolithOS**, then loops a gentle wordmark pulse. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/make-bootanimation.py`. A [frame preview](https://github.com/kvxnomxyz/xonolithos/blob/main/branding/bootanimation-preview.png) is included for review.

Three wallpapers ship inside XonolithSystemCenter: dark Obsidian artwork for tablet and phone, and a bright Glacier tablet wallpaper. The app lets users preview and apply them. Brand assets are source files; a device only receives new artwork in a subsequently built and installed ROM or update package.
